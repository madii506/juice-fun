Real stock perps

Positions live on Hyperliquid's stock markets: TSLA, NVDA, gold, the Nasdaq and pre-IPO names, with live mark prices.

Wins burn the coin

Profit is taken in USDC, swapped back to SOL and used to buy the coin on the market. Those coins are burned on-chain.

Spills refill

A liquidation is a spill, not the end. The coin keeps trading, its fees keep coming, and the next pour opens a fresh position.

Split locked at launch

Set in pump.fun's own fee sharing when the coin launches, with the admin revoked in the same transaction.
